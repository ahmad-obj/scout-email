# WEBERAISE outreach writing rules

- Keep first-touch emails short, specific, natural, and easy to scan.
- Do not sound like a reusable agency template.
- Use no fake compliments and no fake familiarity.
- Do not imply a prior relationship, conversation, recommendation, or knowledge that is not evidenced.
- Prefer one strong, specific observation over a list of website problems.
- Every material observation must be supported by allowed evidence; reasonable inferences must remain cautious and probabilistic.
- Connect the observation to a plausible business implication without inventing private analytics, lost revenue, conversion percentages, or customer behavior.
- Explain WEBERAISE naturally and briefly, only enough to establish relevance.
- Avoid excessive corporate language and common AI-marketing language.
- Avoid generic openings when stronger researched context exists.
- Vary structure across leads rather than forcing every email into one template.
- Never insult the recipient's business, website, team, or existing work.
- Never manufacture urgency, scarcity, guarantees, or results.
- Keep the requested next step proportionate to a cold first contact.
