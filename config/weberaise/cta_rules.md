# WEBERAISE CTA rules

- Use one clear, low-friction, low-pressure CTA.
- A cold first email should invite a small next step, not demand a meeting or lengthy commitment.
- Prefer permission-based language such as asking whether the recipient would like to see an idea, example, or brief suggestion.
- Do not manufacture urgency, deadlines, scarcity, or pressure.
- Do not stack multiple asks in the same email.
- Do not imply the recipient has already agreed to a call, proposal, redesign, audit, or purchase.
- Keep the CTA consistent with the actual WEBERAISE offer and the evidence-backed primary angle.
